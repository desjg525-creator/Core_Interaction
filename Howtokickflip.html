Recipe: How to Kickflip

A kickflip is a skateboarding trick where the board flips underneath you while you jump.

Ingredients

  • 1 skateboard
  • Skate shoes
  • A flat area
  • Helmet and pads
  • Patience

Instructions

  1. Put your back foot on the tail of the skateboard.
  2. Put your front foot slightly behind the front bolts.
  3. Pop the tail down like you are doing an ollie.
  4. Jump up and slide your front foot toward the nose.
  5. Flick your front foot off the edge of the board.
  6. Let the board flip underneath you.
  7. Catch the board with both feet.
  8. Land with your knees bent and keep rolling.

Tips

Practice your ollie first. When you kickflip, focus on jumping up and flicking your front foot off the side of the board.

Finished Recipe

Pop + Flick + Jump + Catch = Kickflip!
