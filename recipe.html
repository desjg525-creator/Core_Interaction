HOW TO KICKFLIP

A simple guide to learning a kickflip.

1. Foot Position

Place your back foot on the tail and your front foot near the middle.

2. Pop

Push down on the tail and jump into the air.

3. Flick

Slide your front foot up and flick off the edge of the board.

4. Catch

Let the board complete one full flip and catch it with your feet.

5. Land

Land on the board with your knees bent.

Keep Practicing!

Wear a helmet and practice somewhere safe.
